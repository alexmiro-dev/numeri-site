Numeri privacy policy

Last updated: 10/11/2026

Who we are

Numeri is a free app to check lottery results, check tickets and organise pools (bolões). The data controller is Miro Studio, Spain, contact: miro.studio.apps@gmail.com. Numeri does not sell tickets, does not accept bets and does not handle money.

What stays only on your device

Your tickets, games, lucky numbers, favourites, balance, photos and ticket readings are stored on your device. QR and ticket-text reading happens on the device (the camera is used only for that). Numeri does not send us this data, except what is described below and that you choose to turn on.

What we may receive

  • Lottery results and estimates: the app downloads public data from our server. In that connection the server sees your IP address, like any website. Our server does not keep the IP address in access logs. Our accounts and pools server records for 30 days the date, method, path, status and the account or device of each request, without the content and without the IP address.
  • App usage and errors (optional, off by default): if you turn it on in "Privacy and app usage", we send events such as screens opened, features used and error codes, with a random device identifier. We never send ticket numbers, names, amounts or typed text. You can turn it off and delete this data inside the app. We keep it for up to 13 months from last use.
  • Satisfaction survey (optional): scores from 1 to 5 per feature and a free comment, linked to the same random identifier. Personal data in the comment (phone, e-mail, ID number) is removed by the server. It is kept until you delete it in the app.
  • Pool (optional): when you create or join a pool, the app sends the server what the pool needs (display name, shares, receipts and, if you provide it, a phone number) to the administrators and participants of that pool. This data is deleted 91 days after the draw takes place.
  • Contacts: the contacts permission is used only if you tap "Invite from contacts" to choose whom to invite. Contacts are not uploaded in bulk.

Ads

The app shows ads from Google AdMob. Google may use the device advertising ID and device data to show and measure ads. Where the law requires it (European Economic Area and United Kingdom), the app asks for your consent and you can review it in "Ad privacy". We limit ad content to the all-audiences rating. Google's policy: https://policies.google.com/technologies/ads

Legal basis and your rights

We process data based on your consent (app usage, survey, ads, pool) and on the legitimate interest of keeping the service secure, in line with the GDPR. You have the right of access, rectification, erasure, withdrawal of consent and to complain to a data protection authority (AEPD in Spain; ANPD in Brazil). To exercise them, write to miro.studio.apps@gmail.com. In the app, "Delete my usage data" deletes what we sent with your identifier.

Children

Numeri is not directed at people under 18 and does not knowingly collect their data.

Transfers and security

Servers are in São Paulo, Brazil (outside the European Economic Area), on a Vultr host. The connection is encrypted (HTTPS).

Changes

If this policy changes, the date above will be updated and, for relevant changes, we will notify you in the app.
